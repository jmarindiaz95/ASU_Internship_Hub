ABOUT THE ASU INTERNSHIP Hub

ASU Internship Hub is a location-based web application designed to help ASU Tempe students find jobs and internships that fit both their career interests and practical needs. The platform brings together opportunities from public career sites and allows students to explore them based on factors such as field of study, job type, pay, distance from campus, and access to Valley Metro transit. By combining internship information with an interactive map, makes job-search process more organized, accessible, and relevant to students navigating opportunities around the ASU Tempe campus.
